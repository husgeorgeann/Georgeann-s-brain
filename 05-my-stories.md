# My Stories

These are Georgeann's own stories, in her own words. Nothing here is invented and nothing is polished past what actually happened. The ugly parts stay in on purpose. That is what makes people believe her.

---

STORY 1: The deal that should have fallen apart
THE MOMENT: A listing with an unusually large number of garages for Lake Havasu, a dated house, a pool, and a casita. It was hard to comp. Georgeann did heavy marketing to validate the pricing and landed a very good offer. Partway through the loan process, the buyer's agent's lender, who had sworn he could do the loan, disappeared. Georgeann worked directly with the buyer's agent to refer the buyer, someone else's client, to a lender she trusted and had worked with before. The deal closed. The buyer was thrilled. The seller was thrilled.
WHAT IT FELT LIKE: Extremely frustrating. The lender had called her several times and assured her repeatedly that he had it handled, and she had passed that reassurance straight on to her seller. Then he vanished.
WHAT IT PROVES: A deal that should have died three times over stayed alive because Georgeann knows her lenders and works with the other side instead of against it, even when the client on the other end of the deal isn't hers.
USE THIS FOR: objection handling on financing fears, working with cooperating agents, About page proof, listing presentations, a video hook about deals that almost fell apart.

STORY 2: No better feeling than their trust
THE MOMENT: Georgeann's California license was used to place investors into interim and hard money loans tied to real estate. It was all numbers, no emotion. Then one of her first transactions as an agent in Arizona was finding a family their dream home, and it was the opposite: all emotion. That family became close, lasting friends. Most of her clients end up becoming personal friends because of the emotion that runs through a real estate transaction.
WHAT IT FELT LIKE: Fulfilling in a way the hard money loans never were. She realized her clients know she cares, that she feels every step of the transaction alongside them, and that there is no better feeling than earning that kind of trust.
WHAT IT PROVES: Georgeann isn't running transactions, she's building relationships that outlast the closing table. The move from purely financial deals to emotionally invested ones is the moment she found what she was actually good at.
USE THIS FOR: About page, video hook about why she got into real estate the way she does it now, client testimonials framing, referral asks, first client conversations.
