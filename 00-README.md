# Georgeann's Business Brain

This folder is the AI Business Brain for Georgeann, a real estate agent. It holds ten files that teach any AI who she is, how she talks, who she serves, and what makes her different.

## The ten files coming

1. `01-identity-and-positioning.md`
2. `02-brand-voice.md`
3. `03-unique-differentiator.md`
4. `04-ideal-client.md`
5. `05-my-stories.md`
6. `06-their-exact-words.md`
7. `07-market-and-competitors.md`
8. `08-compliance.md`
9. `09-wins-and-testimonials.md`
10. `10-best-content.md`

An eleventh file, `11-ai-context.md`, gets generated at the end: the whole brain compressed into something pasteable into any AI, anywhere.
